# Laurus Aroma — Coconut Milk Scrub + Body Butter: edit notes

Two versions, both 9:16 (1080×1920) for Meta Reels/Stories/Feed:

- **60-second cut** for cold audiences
- **Full uncut version (2:21)** for retargeting people who already engaged

## Why we edited it this way

**1. The hook comes from her own content.** The first seconds are what stop the scroll, so we opened on the strongest visual proof in the footage:
- In the 60s cut, the opening is the scrub texture close-up with her line *"Have you ever seen a scrub with this texture?"*
- In the full version, the opening is her before/after hands (one hand treated, one not) with *"I can already see the difference."*

Both hooks use her real words and real results, which reads as authentic UGC rather than an ad.

**2. Her words are never cut mid-sentence.** We transcribed the full Lebanese-Arabic audio and only cut where she naturally pauses at the end of a sentence.
- The 60s cut has just 2 cuts.
- The full version has none.

Choppy jump-cuts make UGC feel fake and hurt trust.

**3. The story follows a clear sales structure:**
1. **Hook:** texture, or the before/after
2. **Problem:** rough skin and "goosebump" skin, especially after shaving or laser
3. **Solution:** a 2-step routine, exfoliate then moisturise, with Laurus Aroma
4. **Product:** coconut milk scrub plus the body butter
5. **Proof:** the hand comparison, scrub + butter vs. nothing
6. **CTA:** "get them from Laurus Aroma", plus an "Order now" button

**4. Full captions in her dialect.** Most people watch Meta ads with the sound off. Every line is captioned in Lebanese Arabic, timed to her speech, and placed clear of Meta's on-screen buttons.

**5. Minimal, consistent graphics.** All graphics share one style and appear exactly when she says the matching words, so they support her message instead of competing with it:
- the step cards (1 Exfoliate → 2 Moisturise)
- the brand name
- the coconut-milk badge
- the "body butter" card
- labels on the hands

**6. A visual focus on the proof.** During the hand comparison, a soft spotlight follows the hand she is talking about, first the treated hand and then the bare one, so the viewer sees the difference immediately.

**7. Smooth, premium motion.**
- one slow, continuous camera drift instead of jumpy zooms
- soft dissolves between shots in the 60s cut
- a warm, gentle colour grade

**8. Music and sound design.**
- A calm, premium instrumental bed that automatically lowers under her voice.
- Soft sound effects (a gentle hit on the hook, light pops when graphics appear, a chime on the CTA) add polish without being distracting.
- Loudness is set to -14 LUFS, the standard for social platforms.

## There was a problem in the original footage, and it is fixed

The original recording had a **lip-sync issue**: the audio was about **70–100 milliseconds behind her lips**, so she appeared to start speaking before the sound came out. This came from the source recording itself, not from the edit.

We measured it by comparing her mouth movement with the speech audio across the whole video. We then re-aligned the audio and picture by 80 ms in both versions. After the fix, both final videos measure **0 ms** offset, so lips and voice now match.
